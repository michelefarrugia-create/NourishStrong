<product_requirements>
The product, NourishStrong, is a web-based wellness application designed to support users on their weight loss journey by focusing on mindful eating and positive reinforcement rather than quantitative metrics.

**Core Problem:** Traditional calorie-tracking apps can foster unhealthy obsessions with numbers. NourishStrong aims to solve this by providing a supportive environment where users track meals and activities without seeing calorie, macro, or weight data. This sensitive information is accessible only to assigned coaches or supporters, enabling them to provide informed guidance.

**Key Implemented Features:**
*   **Role-Based Access:** Separate dashboards and data visibility for 'Users' and 'Coaches'.
*   **AI Meal Logging:** Users can take or upload a photo of their meal. An AI (powered by GPT-4o via Emergent LLM Key) analyzes the image to estimate portions, calories, and macros, which are stored for the coach's view.
*   **Gamification:** Includes a streak tracker, achievement badges, progress celebrations, and daily challenges.
*   **Analytics (for Coaches):** Coaches can view progress charts, weekly summaries, and meal patterns for their clients.
*   **Activity & Health Tracking:** Manual logging for daily activities and a file-upload feature to sync data from Apple Health (XML) and Google Fit (CSV).
*   **User Profiles:** A dedicated profile page where users can manage their info and upload a profile picture. Weight is managed exclusively by the coach.
*   **Privacy-First Design:** All quantitative nutritional and weight data is hidden from the user's view.
</product_requirements>
